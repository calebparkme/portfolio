Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how this personal booking automation system ("the System") collects, uses, and protects information, including SMS/text messaging data.

Information We Collect

The System collects a single mobile phone number, provided directly by the account owner for the sole purpose of sending automated booking-related notifications to that number.

How We Use Your Information

Mobile phone numbers and SMS opt-in data are never shared, sold, or disclosed to any third party or affiliate for marketing purposes. Phone numbers are used solely to deliver booking confirmations, appointment reminders, and schedule change alerts to the account owner.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on booking activity. Recipients may receive occasional notifications related to bookings and appointment changes. Message and data rates may apply.

Opting Out

Recipients may opt out of receiving messages at any time by replying STOP. Reply HELP for assistance.

Data Retention and Security

Phone numbers and related data are stored securely and retained only as long as necessary to provide notification services.
