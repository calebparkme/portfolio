About This Service

Calebs Notification is a booking notification service that delivers automated SMS alerts about class and appointment reservations. Recipients receive text messages when a booking is confirmed, cancelled, or rescheduled.

What Messages Look Like

Calebs Notification: Booked - Cardio Strength (MLC) with Lilly (#111664) on 2026/08/21 09:15 am - 10:05 am. Reply STOP to opt out.

Message Details

  • Message frequency varies based on booking activity.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP to opt out at any time.
  • Reply HELP for assistance.
  • Messages are transactional only — no marketing or promotional content is sent.
